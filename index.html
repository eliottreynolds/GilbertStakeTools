Leader Tools

Gilbert Arizona Stake

This site supports a small set of internal tools built for leaders of the Gilbert Arizona Stake of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The tools are private and require sign-in; this page exists so that people and services encountering them have somewhere to read about what they are.

The Rising Generation

A dashboard that gathers indicators already recorded in Church systems — temple recommend status, priesthood progression, seminary enrollment and attendance, and class attendance — and presents them to stake and ward leaders so that a young person who may need support is noticed early.

It is a read-only view of existing records. It stores no assessments, keeps no history of individuals, and is accessible only to specifically authorized leaders.

Who can use these tools

Access is limited to named members of the stake presidency, bishoprics, and clerks, each maintained on an explicit list. Signing in with a Google account that is not on that list grants nothing.

Privacy

Sign-in uses Google to confirm a leader's email address, and nothing else. See the privacy policy for details.
