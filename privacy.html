Privacy Policy

Gilbert Arizona Stake Leader Tools · Updated 23 November 2026

These tools are private, internal applications used by leaders of the Gilbert Arizona Stake of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. They are not offered to the public and cannot be used by anyone who has not been explicitly authorized.

What we request from your Google account

When you sign in, we ask Google for one thing: your email address. Specifically, the openid and email scopes.

We do not request, receive, or have any access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, photos, or any other Google service or data.

What we do with it

Your email address is compared against a list of leaders authorized to use the tool. If it appears on that list, you are shown the information appropriate to your calling. If it does not, you are shown nothing.

Your address is held in temporary server memory for the duration of your session — at most six hours — and then discarded. It is not written to any permanent store, not used for analytics, and not used to contact you.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not transfer your information to any third party, except Google itself as the sign-in provider.
  • We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not place advertising or third-party tracking on these tools.

Information shown inside the tools

The tools display membership and participation records that already exist in the Church's own systems, to leaders who are already entitled to see them as part of their calling. No such information is published, shared outside those leaders, or used for any purpose beyond supporting the members of this stake.

Retention and removal

Session records expire automatically within six hours. To have your access removed, or to ask what is held about you, contact the maintainer below; removal takes effect immediately.

Contact
